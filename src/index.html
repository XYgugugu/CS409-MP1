The Legend of Luo Xiaohei (罗小黑战记)

This has been my favorite animation ever since its first release in 2011.

ABOUT

罗小黑战记 (The Legend of Luo Xiaohei) is a flash animation and webtoon series developed by Chinese artist MTJJ. It began in 2011 and quickly gained a cult following for its unique aesthetic, charming characters, and heartfelt storytelling.

The story follows Luo Xiaohei, a cat spirit navigating a world between humans and the supernatural. The animation explores deep themes of identity and freedom while staying accessible through expressive visuals and lovable characters.
